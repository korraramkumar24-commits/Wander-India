India's History

Stories of civilizations, kingdoms and independence

ANCIENT ERA

Indus Valley Civilization

The Indus Valley Civilization was one of the earliest urban civilizations in the world. It developed advanced cities, drainage systems, trade networks and crafts.

MEDIEVAL ERA

Kingdoms & Architecture

Medieval India witnessed the rise of powerful kingdoms, forts, palaces, temples and remarkable architectural achievements.

MODERN INDIA

Independence & Republic

India's independence movement brought together people from many regions. India became independent in 1947 and became a republic in 1950.
